# Modular Observation Quad V2 — AD5M Production

Designed around the FlashForge Adventurer 5M and the existing 215 mm maximum-part rule.

## Printed inventory
Only **three unique printable designs** are maintained:
- 1 × center plate
- 4 × identical replaceable arms
- 4 × identical optional prop guards

A damaged arm or guard can therefore be reprinted individually instead of replacing the complete airframe.

## Assembly
Each arm uses one M3 retention fastener at the center plate. The electronics mount to the center plate using the 20×20 mm pattern. Battery straps pass through the integrated slots. The 1404-class motors use the finalized 9×9 mm M2 pattern.

## Print orientation
All pieces print flat with broad faces on the bed. The geometry intentionally avoids support-dependent overhangs. Start with 0.20 mm layers. PETG is preferred for arms/guards where impact resistance matters; PLA is suitable for initial fit checks.

## Production rule
Print one center plate and one arm first for a fit check. Once verified, duplicate the identical arm four times. Guards are optional and identical.

This remains an observation/tracking platform; it has no physical interception mechanism.
