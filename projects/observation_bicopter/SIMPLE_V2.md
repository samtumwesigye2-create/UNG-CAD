# UNG Simple Bicopter V2

Simplified for the FlashForge Adventurer 5M.

## Unique printable designs
1. Center body — quantity 1
2. Identical arm — quantity 2
3. Identical optional guard — quantity 2

All three designs are intended to print flat. The arm combines the structural boom, motor pad and a generic servo pocket so there is no separate printed tilt-pod component.

## Important production gate
The servo pocket is deliberately **not production-final**. Its current 24 x 13 mm envelope is only a placeholder. The exact servo body, output-shaft position, horn clearance and motor-tilt linkage must be matched to the selected servo before flight hardware is printed.

The motor interface remains 9 x 9 mm M2. The body retains a 20 x 20 mm flight-controller pattern and battery strap slots.

Print one body and one arm as the first fit test after the servo interface is finalized.
