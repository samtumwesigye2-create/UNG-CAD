# Simple Bicopter V2 — Servo Interface

Selected reference servo class: **EMAX ES09MD II digital metal-gear micro servo**.

Reference body dimensions are approximately **23 x 12 x 24.5 mm**, operating at **4.8–6.0 V**, with published torque around **2.3 kg·cm at 4.8 V / 2.6 kg·cm at 6 V**.

CAD pocket: **23.6 x 12.6 mm** through the arm, providing about 0.3 mm nominal clearance per side for FDM fit.

The output horn/linkage and its travel stops still require a physical fit check before flight testing. Do not assume the nominal pocket alone validates the thrust-vector mechanism.
