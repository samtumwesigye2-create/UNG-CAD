# UNG-CAD Construction Takeoff and Sequencing — prototype

A standalone **module within the existing UNG-CAD repository**, not a new service or Railway project.

Open `construction-takeoff/index.html` in a browser. Works on phones and desktops without dependencies.

Includes schematic exploded construction visualization, phase slider and playback, illustrative material quantities and unit-rate costs, adjustable estimate multiplier, and CSV export.

**Important:** All building geometry, quantities and prices are synthetic illustrative placeholders. This is not BIM import, actual CAD quantity extraction, structural analysis, procurement integration, or certified estimating. Do not rely on outputs for bids, construction, safety, or purchases.

Future integration requires reading real CAD/BIM entities and measurements, associating quantity rules and assemblies, versioned local price catalogs, scope and waste assumptions, building-code review, and the existing UNG Manufacturing Gatekeeper. No automatic Railway deployment is required.
