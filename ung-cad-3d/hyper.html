Hyper: print 4D & 5D shapes

A printer only moves in 3 directions, so a 4D or 5D shape is printed as its shadow (projected into 3D, like a cube's shadow on a wall) or as a slice (cut with a flat 3D knife). Turning it in the extra directions W and V gives a different printable shape every time.
